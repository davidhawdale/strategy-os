# [Press &amp; Journal](https://www.pressandjournal.co.uk/) — Subscription Product Briefing

**Date:** 2026-05-05
**Sources:** Live website screenshots (2026-05-05), App Store listing, Pugpig/DC Thomson press material, Press Gazette, PressReader, DC Thomson shop.

---

## What is it?

A regional daily print newspaper (six days/week, tabloid) with four distinct digital product components. The print newspaper is premium — paid only, no free access:

- **Website** — [pressandjournal.co.uk](https://www.pressandjournal.co.uk/). Split between free and premium content. A metered paywall applies — exact article limit and reset period not confirmed.
- **App** — iOS and Android. Launched mid-2024. Live news, breaking alerts, personalised feeds, and puzzles. The app includes links to the ePaper and nudges to buy and also links directly to press and journal pages for podcasts and newsletters.
- **ePaper** — a digital replica of the daily edition of the Press and Journal, published six days a week. Available on smartphone, tablet, and desktop.
- **Email newsletters** — a significant range of localised and interest-based newsletters (see Newsletters section below). Free to sign up; account required.
- **Podcasts** — produced in small volume alongside the main editorial output.
- **[Facebook](facebook.com/thepressjournal)** — driving to the website.
- **[X](https://x.com/pressjournal)** - driving to the website

## Tiers and Pricing

Three digital tiers are currently live on the [website](pressandjournal.co.uk/subscribe/):

| Tier                                  | Includes                                                                                                        | Monthly    | Annual      | Annual saving |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ----------- | ------------- |
| **Sport Pack**                  | Sport content only; personalised newsletter; subscriber events                                                  | £4.99/mo  | £49.99/yr  | £9.98        |
| **Web Pack** *(most popular)* | Full website including puzzles; personalised newsletter; subscriber events; live news and alerts in the P&J App | £8.99/mo  | £89.99/yr  | £17.98       |
| **Web + ePaper**                | Everything in Web Pack + digital ePaper on all devices                                                          | £23.99/mo | £239.99/yr | £47.99       |

All tiers are cancel-anytime, renewing at the same rate.

**Print (home delivery)** is sold separately through the DC Thomson print shop, with regional edition selection required. Indicative pricing ~£610/yr — treat as provisional. The shop URL has not been confirmed; dctdigital.com appears to be a staging environment only.

**Print and digital are sold separately.** No print+digital bundle has been found.

## Pricing Discrepancies

Two pages on the live P&J website quote different prices for what appears to be the same top-tier product (website + app + digital edition/ePaper), captured on the same date:

| Source                                | Description                                                                                  | Price                |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Main subscribe page (`/subscribe/`) | "Web + ePaper Monthly — Web + App + ePaper"                                                 | **£23.99/mo** |
| ePaper promotional banner             | "Full access to our website and app, including the digital edition of The Press and Journal" | **£21.99/mo** |

The £2/month difference is unexplained. Possible causes: an outdated promotional page not yet updated to reflect a price rise; an A/B pricing test; a legacy introductory rate on the ePaper-specific landing page; or a genuinely different product variant despite near-identical descriptions. The discrepancy should be treated as unresolved until confirmed directly with DC Thomson.

## Sister Title — Evening Express

The Evening Express is a DC Thomson Aberdeen city paper published alongside the P&J. The two titles are related but distinct products:

|                             | Press & Journal                                   | Evening Express                                  |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Coverage**          | Northern Scotland — 9 geographic areas           | Aberdeen city only                               |
| **Focus**             | Regional news, politics, sport, lifestyle         | Local Aberdeen news; strong Aberdeen FC coverage |
| **Print circulation** | 21,765 (H1 2024)                                  | 9,449 (2023)                                     |
| **Website**           | pressandjournal.co.uk                             | Merged into pressandjournal.co.uk (Nov 2021)     |
| **App**               | P&J App (Pugpig Bolt, 2024)                       | Separate Evening Express app (iOS and Android)   |
| **ePaper reader**     | Within P&J app + browser reader                   | epaper.eveningexpress.co.uk + dedicated app      |
| **Subscription**      | Three tiers — Sport Pack, Web Pack, Web + ePaper | Single ePaper-focused subscription               |
| **Price**             | £4.99–£23.99/mo                                | £14.99/mo / £144.99/yr                         |

**Key distinction:** Because the Evening Express website was merged into pressandjournal.co.uk, its digital identity is primarily the ePaper and its dedicated app. It does not have a separate web tier or sport-only tier. The two titles share a website but have separate apps and separate subscriptions.

**Supplements:** The Evening Express ePaper includes The Aberdonian and Seven magazine.
