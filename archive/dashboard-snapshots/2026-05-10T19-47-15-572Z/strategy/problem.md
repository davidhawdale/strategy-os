## Problem or Opportunity
Local news consumers in the Scottish Borders have only a weekly cadence of local news - unlike in Aberdeen and the north where D C Thompson run a successful press and journal which has which offers local print editions, and an app to deliver local news to consumers on a much more frequent basis. Would a similar would a similar service be as valuable to consumers in the borders as it is to in Aberdeen in the north?

## Goals
A better, or equivalent, financial outcome, as is delivered by for D C Thompson, as is delivered by the Press and Journal in the north.

## Capabilities and Resources
At this stage, we're only interested in investigating the business case. So we have resources for research and very basic build and prototyping facilities, as well as AI.

## Other Constraints
