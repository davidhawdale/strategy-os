# Getting to Sell Ready

Sell ready is the system's green light for execution. Until it's true, no money gets spent and no downstream teams get activated.

It requires four things to all be true at once:

1. The **Problem** hypothesis is at least Researched
2. The **Segment** hypothesis is at least Researched
3. No **HIGH-blast assumption** is unresolved
4. No **architecture contradiction** exists in the register

**Hypotheses move up through confidence states** — Unvalidated, Researched, Supported — as evidence is gathered. Problem and Segment only need to reach Researched, which requires secondary research or synthesised reasoning. They don't need ground-truth interviews yet.

**Assumptions are the bets the strategy is built on.** Each one is rated by blast radius — how much breaks if it turns out to be wrong. HIGH blast means the strategy collapses. Any HIGH-blast assumption that hasn't been tested blocks sell ready, regardless of which hypothesis it belongs to. The way to clear them is to gather evidence — interviews, experiments, data requests — until enough are resolved that none remain blocking.

**Architecture contradictions** are inconsistencies within the register itself — places where two sections make incompatible claims. They don't require external research to fix, just someone updating the document to make it internally consistent.

Once all four conditions pass, the Gap Definer re-runs, confirms the predicate, and sets sell ready to true. At that point execution can begin.

## Next Actions

1. **Fix any architecture contradictions** — check the register for sections that make incompatible claims and align them. No research required, can be done immediately.

2. **Identify your HIGH-blast assumptions** — open the Gap Ledger and filter for HIGH blast radius, OPEN status. These are your blockers.

3. **Design evidence to resolve them** — for each HIGH-blast assumption, determine the cheapest test that would either confirm or kill it. Usually a small number of structured interviews or a data request covers most of them.

4. **Raise escalations for anything that requires a decision or resource** — fieldwork costs money and needs authorisation. Get those decisions made before designing the research programme.

5. **Run the Gap Definer** after evidence is gathered — it re-evaluates the predicate, resolves assumptions against the new evidence, and sets sell ready if the conditions pass.
