# Strategy Seed

Date: 2026-05-10
Mode: EXTENSION

## Future State
Could DC Thomson create a trusted, financially sustainable daily local news service for the Scottish Borders?

## Current Reality
The Borders appears to have fragmented weekly local news provision, while DC Thomson has a working regional daily model in the Press and Journal.

## Strategic Bet
The Press and Journal model, or a modified version of it, may be transferable to the Scottish Borders despite the region’s smaller population and town fragmentation.

## Validation Standard
It is a good bet if resident demand, advertiser appetite, town identity, transferability, and unit economics hold at Borders scale. It is a bad bet if pain is weak, the market is too small, towns reject a shared product, or the economics only work in larger regions.
