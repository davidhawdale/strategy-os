## What Was Researched

- **Scottish Borders demographics**: population ~117K (stable, aging), fragmented town structure — largest towns Hawick (13,586), Galashiels (12,302), Peebles (8,581), Kelso (6,861), Jedburgh (3,832); dozens of smaller settlements; ~80% superfast broadband coverage; significant offline subset 60+.
- **Competitive landscape**: Border Telegraph (Newsquest, weekly Wednesdays) and Southern Reporter (also weekly) are the only regional titles. No daily product exists. Newsquest Scotland recently passed 50K digital subscribers across all titles.
- **Press and Journal model**: DC Thomson's regional daily — six geographic editions, ~21–28K circulation, paywalled digital at £10–15/month, >50K DCT-wide digital subscribers, £41.1m subscriptions revenue to March 2025.
- **UK regional press context**: 293+ closures since 2005; H1 2025 regional dailies down ~18% in print; industry revenue declining 6.1% CAGR. Ad-only models structurally broken.
- **Transferability analogs**: Mill Media (Manchester Mill, Sheffield Tribune, Liverpool Post) — £7/month, ~10K paid subs combined, broke even 2023. Bristol Cable membership model — 2,500 paying. Both prove sub-million-population markets can sustain quality local journalism with subscription-led models.
- **Local advertising**: UK SME local ad spend £100–£2,000/week per business; structural decline in print ad budgets.

## What Survived Compression

- **Problem (P1)**: "No daily regional product" — the gap is in frequency and aggregation, not existence of journalism. Alternatives P2 (depth/quality) and P4 (civic accountability) carried as alternative framings.
- **Segment (S1)**: Engaged Borders residents 40+ as primary. S3 (SME owners) carried as co-primary (dual role as readers and advertisers). S4 (diaspora) carried as expansion segment.
- **Value Proposition (VP1)**: "The whole Borders, every day" — daily regional with town editions. VP2 (newsletter-first Mill-style) and VP3 (civic accountability flavour) carried as alternatives.
- **Unit Economics (UE1)**: Subscription-led hybrid with print weekend edition. UE2 (digital-only) carried as fallback. UE4 (membership) as commercial overlay.
