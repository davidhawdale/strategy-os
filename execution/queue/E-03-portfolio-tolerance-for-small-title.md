# E-03 — DCT portfolio tolerance for a sub-scale title

**Type:** Values decision (governor only)
**Raised by:** Strategist (BUILD pass)
**Date:** 2026-05-10

## Decision Needed

Confirm DC Thomson's portfolio tolerance for a title whose realistic ceiling may be 6,000–8,000 paying subscribers (smaller than P&J or Courier). Is a contribution-positive but small title strategically valued, or does it need to scale to a higher floor to justify management attention?

## Why system cannot decide

This is a values decision about portfolio strategy that only the governor (or DCT leadership) can answer. It directly affects how the kill condition for Unit Economics should be calibrated.

## Options

- **A. Accept small-but-contribution-positive as success** — kill conditions remain as drafted (£90K loss at month 18 is the trigger).
- **B. Require P&J-scale ambitions (15K+ subs)** — current base case is insufficient; need a more aggressive growth model or larger geographic frame (e.g. Borders + Dumfries & Galloway).
- **C. Treat as defensive/civic-purpose play** — different success criteria entirely.

## What is at stake

Option B changes the segment hypothesis (need a larger addressable base than the Borders alone), the VP (must compete across South of Scotland), and the unit economics threshold.

## Recommendation

Governor decision — Strategist holds no view on portfolio strategy.
