# E-01 — Borders fieldwork mandate (resident + SME)

**Type:** Ground-truth gap blocking strategy progression
**Raised by:** Strategist (BUILD pass)
**Date:** 2026-05-10

## Decision Needed

Authorise and resource ground-truth fieldwork in the Scottish Borders before any pilot launch budget is committed. Specifically: structured interviews with 20+ residents across 4+ towns, an online survey via Borders Facebook groups, and 10+ SME advertiser interviews.

## Why system cannot decide

This is a values + resource decision. The Strategist can construct hypotheses from public data but cannot generate the T1 ground-truth required to move Problem and Segment from RESEARCHED to SUPPORTED. Without it, the entire register remains contingent.

## Options

- **A. Fund full fieldwork (~£15K–£40K + 8–12 weeks)** — properly validates pain, segment size, willingness-to-pay, and SME advertiser appetite. Recommended.
- **B. Light-touch fieldwork (~£5K, 4 weeks)** — survey-only, no interviews. Faster, weaker evidence; will not resolve T3 assumptions.
- **C. Skip fieldwork; go straight to a small pilot** — treats the pilot itself as the validation. Higher capital risk; faster signal but the signal is conflated with execution quality.

## What is at stake

Three load-bearing T3 assumptions (resident pain, segment size with WTP, SME advertising appetite) are HIGH blast radius and currently unvalidated. Proceeding without fieldwork commits DCT capital to a pilot whose base case may not hold.

## Recommendation

Option A.
