# T-02 — Borders SME advertiser fieldwork

**Reduces gaps:** G-02 (UNIT_ECONOMICS / ECONOMIC_VIABILITY).
**Linked escalation:** E-01.
**Evidence target:** T1.
**Owner:** Governor.
**Deadline (recommended):** 2026-07-31.
**Status:** OPEN — pending E-01 authorisation.

## Objective

Generate T1 ground-truth on whether Borders SMEs will sustain aggregate £150K–£400K/yr advertising spend across the proposed product, and whether the reader/advertiser dual-role assumption holds.

## Method

1. Structured interviews with at least 10 Borders SMEs, stratified across 5 sectors (hospitality, retail, professional services, agriculture, property) and 3+ towns.
2. Recruitment via Borders Chamber of Commerce, Borders Scottish Business Club, direct outreach, and snowball referrals.
3. Problem-first protocol: establish current advertising spend, channel mix, satisfaction, and pains **before** introducing the proposed product. Pricing test at the end.

## Required Outputs

- Current annual ad spend per SME and channel mix.
- Satisfaction with current channels (Border Telegraph, Southern Reporter, Facebook, Google).
- Willingness to commit a recurring monthly budget at £100–£2,000/week tiers.
- Implied aggregate ad revenue at observed willingness × addressable SME count.
- Reader/advertiser overlap: do interviewed SME owners also subscribe to / read local news? (Segment §2 Assumption 4 reference.)

## Success Signals

- ≥20% express willingness to commit a recurring budget.
- Implied aggregate ≥£150K/yr.
- ≥50% reader/advertiser overlap (dual-role assumption holds).

## Failure Signals

- <15% interest → UE advertising-side base case collapses; escalate immediately.
- Disjoint reader/advertiser sets → trigger sales-motion redesign (separate hire from Day 1).

## Validity Constraints

- Do not pitch a media buy. This is research, not sales.
- Do not present DCT-branded materials until after current-spend section completes.

## Budget envelope (per E-01 Option A — combined with T-01)

Shared with T-01 envelope: £15K–£40K, 8–12 weeks elapsed.
