# T-01 — Borders resident fieldwork

**Reduces gaps:** G-01 (PROBLEM / PAIN_CLARITY), G-03 (SEGMENT / SEGMENT_CLARITY).
**Linked escalation:** E-01.
**Evidence target:** T1.
**Owner:** Governor.
**Deadline (recommended):** 2026-07-31.
**Status:** OPEN — pending E-01 authorisation.

## Objective

Generate T1 ground-truth on whether engaged Borders residents experience the absence of a daily, regional, professionally-edited local news product as an acute information frustration; and whether the segment proxies and addressable count assumed in the register hold.

## Method

1. Structured interviews with at least 20 Borders residents, stratified across **at least 4 towns** (minimum: Hawick, Galashiels, Peebles, Kelso or Jedburgh). Mix recruitment via Borders Facebook groups, market-town intercept, and partner channels (Live Borders, council, BSC, BCofC).
2. Online survey via Borders Facebook groups and partner email lists. Target ≥200 responses, stratified by town and age band.
3. Problem-first protocol: questions establish current information habits, frustrations, and workarounds **before** any product concept is introduced. VP concept-card preference test (G-06) sequenced at the **end** of each session only.

## Required Outputs

- % of respondents naming "lack of daily local news" in top-5 information frustrations (Problem kill condition reference: <30% = kill).
- % stating willingness to pay ≥£6/month for a daily Borders product, by age band.
- Distribution of preferred format: digital, print, hybrid.
- Recognition of "DC Thomson" and "Press and Journal" brands.
- Town vs regional framing preference (Segment + VP kill condition reference: dominant town-only with rejection of regional = kill).
- Correlation between segment proxies (BT/SR readership, Borders FB group membership, TD/EH postcode) and proposed engaged-resident behaviours (council engagement, business engagement).

## Success Signals (return to Gap Definer)

- ≥30% pain frequency on daily-news absence.
- ≥10% WTP at ≥£6/month.
- ≥60% brand recognition for DCT/P&J.
- Segment proxies correlate as hypothesised.

## Failure Signals (return immediately)

- Dominant town-only framing with explicit rejection of regional product.
- <10% WTP at any monthly price.

## Validity Constraints (Solution Contamination Rule)

- Do not lead with DCT branding or product concept.
- Do not present a finished product; concept-card preference test only after problem section completes.
- Interviewer must not solution-lead — record verbatim where possible.

## Budget envelope (per E-01 Option A)

£15K–£40K, 8–12 weeks elapsed.
