# E-02 — Internal DCT cost-allocation and shared-services data

**Type:** Internal data gap (governor can resolve via internal channels)
**Raised by:** Strategist (BUILD pass)
**Date:** 2026-05-10

## Decision Needed

Obtain DC Thomson internal numbers on (a) shared-service marginal cost of a new sub-scale title, (b) cross-promotion CPM/conversion data from P&J and Courier subscriber funnels, (c) actual Press and Journal Borders-area readership/subscriber baseline if it exists.

## Why system cannot decide

These data live inside DCT and are not publicly available. The unit economics base case currently assumes shared-services materially reduce marginal newsroom cost, but the assumption is T2.

## Options

- **A. Request data from DCT finance, ad ops, and subscription teams.**
- **B. Build the model with industry-benchmark proxies and flag a CHALLENGE-mode update later.**

## What is at stake

If shared services do not scale down to a sub-15K-subscriber title without disproportionate central overhead, the entire UE1 (subscription-led hybrid) model could collapse to UE2 (digital-only newsletter) — a different solution shape.

## Recommendation

Option A. This is a high-leverage internal ask.
