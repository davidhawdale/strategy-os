**Reduces gaps:** G-04 (UNIT_ECONOMICS / shared-services), G-08 (GTM / CAC validation).
**Linked escalation:** E-02.
**Evidence target:** T1 (internal data).
**Owner:** Governor (route into DCT finance, ad ops, subscriptions).
**Deadline (recommended):** 2026-07-31.
**Status:** OPEN — pending E-02 authorisation.

Note: this task is associated with deferred gaps G-04 and G-08, not the top-3 active set. It is included in this cycle because it is an escalation-led data request that the governor must initiate; it does not consume the team's gap-reduction capacity beyond the request itself. If E-02 is rejected, this task is closed and the base case continues to rely on T2 industry proxies.

## Objective

Obtain DC Thomson internal data sufficient to firm the Unit Economics base case and validate Phase 1 CAC assumptions.

## Required Data

1. **Shared-services marginal cost model:** what is the additional fully-loaded cost of operating one new sub-15K-subscriber title across (subs platform, ad ops, CMS, distribution, finance/HR)? Express as £/subscriber/year or fixed + variable.
2. **Cross-promotion performance:** for P&J and Courier digital subscriber funnels, what is the historical conversion rate of cross-promo impressions to free-tier sign-up and to paid subscription? CPM equivalent if available.
3. **Borders-area readership baseline:** any existing P&J or Courier digital subscriber count within Borders postcodes (TD1–TD15, EH44–EH46). Provides a floor for the segment hypothesis.
4. **Editorial cost benchmark:** loaded cost of a journalist FTE in DCT's Scottish operation; loaded cost of a regional editor; standard shared-back-office allocation percentage for a new title.

## Success Signals

- Data confirms shared-services scale down to sub-15K title with overhead ≤20% of revenue → UE base case holds.
- Cross-promo conversion ≥2% impression-to-free-tier and ≥3% free-to-paid → GTM Phase 1 CAC assumption holds.

## Failure Signals

- Shared-services overhead at sub-15K title >35% of revenue → UE1 hybrid model collapses to UE2 newsletter; trigger Strategist re-cast.
- Cross-promo conversion <1% → Phase 1 CAC rises 20–40%; GTM re-plan.

## Out of scope

This is not a request for DCT investment commitment. It is a data request only.
